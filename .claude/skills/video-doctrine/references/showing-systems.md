# Showing a System Instead of Labelling It

Replace "connect", "optimise", "scale" with visible operations. This is the pillar v1
failed hardest: its opening beat put the words *"Your business runs on chaos"* over six
evenly-spaced, perfectly still grey dots. The word did all the work and the picture
actively contradicted it.

## Data flow as a sequence of states

1. inputs arrive as separate particles
2. they are classified by colour, direction or shape
3. they route through a central mechanism
4. bottlenecks clear
5. outputs emerge in a coherent pattern

Nobody needs to read "our platform optimises workflows". They watch disorder become
coordinated movement.

## Particles as agents, not decoration

Each one needs an origin, a destination, a path, a speed, a state, a response to
obstacles, and an arrival behaviour. A convincing routing sequence has particles waiting
or searching, a path illuminating ahead of them, acceleration into the route, a node
changing state on arrival, and a downstream response.

**Vary speed and spacing.** Identical particles at identical intervals is a screensaver.

## State changes

dormant→active · fragmented→grouped · noisy→filtered · manual→automated · hidden→revealed
· disconnected→synchronised · flat→dimensional

Animate several properties at once: colour shifts, shape morphs, brightness rises, motion
becomes ordered, blur resolves, lines connect, local particles reorganise. A "before" and
"after" label is weaker than showing the RULES change.

## Before/after morph

- before: scattered, low contrast, irregular timing
- transition: forces pull elements into alignment
- after: coherent grid, shared rhythm, strong central axis

Keep some identity across the two so the viewer knows the second came from the first.
D1's six vendors collapsing into one mark and then radiating eight specialists is exactly
this shape, and it is the argument the deck is making.

## Constraint visualisation

A path avoids obstacles. A chaotic field snaps to a grid. Multiple inputs converge without
collision. A route reroutes when a node goes down. This shows intelligence without any
copy at all.

## Type as the system's OUTPUT

Do not place a headline over the process. Let the process produce it: routes converge into
letterforms, particles assemble a word, a moving data field cuts a mask, the interface
expands and its labels become the message. Then the words are earned.

## The honesty constraint

We had no footage of anyone else's tools, so D2's six vendor screens are DARK AND EMPTY
frames with names on them. Dressing our own product recordings up as a competitor's would
be a fabrication. The honest version turned out to be the stronger picture anyway: six
things you pay for and never look at.
